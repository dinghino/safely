# Places Explore

This slice of the app allows user to explore areas dynamically though a map view
and see what places are available with a more classic view, through lists of
categorized POIs and filters.

It is meant to allow people to find things methodically, without having to rely directly on the map
and clicking every single item in the viewport to see what they are.

Works as a good entry point for users to get an overview of the area and what is available.

It also works for us developers to work on individual pieces for the future `/map`
section where we'll compose our views similar to how google maps and other apps work.
