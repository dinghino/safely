This section of the documentation covers the core concepts, entities and the general
philosophy behind the architecture of Safely.

While this is mostly for developers, it can also be useful to users to understand
how Safely works under the hood and what everything means.

We'll cover what are the main entities and how they relate to each other, operating
modes and other important concepts that will help you understand and work with the
system better.
